# Discussion

Exploratory design notes and proposals that are **not yet committed requirements**.

Documents here are for thinking through future capabilities before they earn a place
in `PRD.md` / `PLANNING.md`. They are speculative by design — open questions,
trade-offs, and feasibility sketches live here. When a discussion matures into agreed
scope, fold the decisions into the PRD/PLANNING and link back to the discussion doc.

Each document should state its **status** (Discussion / Proposed / Superseded) and date
at the top.
